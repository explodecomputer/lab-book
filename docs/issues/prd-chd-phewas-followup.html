User stories

  1. As an analyst, I can reconstruct complete exact mediator and outcome associations from existing local inputs with correct P values, stable IDs and provenance.
  2. As an analyst, every mediator’s comparator excludes all 41 known CHD leads and their regions, including leads without mediator pairs or outcome associations, and is identical across that mediator’s exact-hit tests and decomposition.
  3. As a reader, I can inspect one comparison panel per mediator with one other-SNP estimate and every retained exact hit, readable labels/CIs/counts and per-hit heterogeneity results.
  4. As a reader, I can inspect signed total, indirect and model-based residual components, their uncertainty and explicit unavailable components.
  5. As a reader, I can inspect one corrected pooled estimate per mediator, identical to its shared comparator, with instrument/exclusion counts and multiplicity adjustment.
  6. As a maintainer, I can reuse one outcome-neutral implementation by supplying associations, an explicit complete lead inventory, outcome metadata and output settings, with CHD-appropriate units and no dependency on a developer’s checkout path.
  7. As an analyst, I can run the notebook/report offline to regenerate the three figures, their PDF/PNG exports and source CSVs, with fresh computation and documented dependencies.
  8. As a reader, I see separate BH test families, instrument/missingness diagnostics and scientifically limited interpretations rather than claims that heterogeneity proves a direct pathway or that nonsignificance proves mediation.

Scientific contract

Use the exact named SNP for each target, never a region aggregate or a nearest-SNP proxy. Identify pairs by mediator GWAS ID and SNP, with display labels separate. Require consistent allele orientation and valid associations, conservatively reject A/T and C/G palindromes, handle conflicting duplicates explicitly and report unusable targets without dropping inventory rows.

Exclude the full union of all 41 CHD lead SNPs and inclusive ±1 Mb regions before outcome matching and harmonisation. The lead-coordinate inventory is independent of PheWAS returns and outcome availability. Validate coordinates/build consistency; report uncertain/conflicting coordinates rather than inventing positions. Do not use a union of per-target complements. Reuse one comparator per mediator across Q tests, pooled MR and decomposition. Single-instrument estimates use Wald ratios; multi-instrument estimates use IVW with multiplicative random effects, following the reference implementation.

For each pair define aligned a=G→mediator, t=G→CHD outcome, b=shared mediator→CHD estimate, i=a*b and d=t-i. Report signed components in outcome units per recorded target effect allele, never sum across correlated mediators or present mediation percentages. Under the explicit zero-covariance working assumption, Var(i)=b²Var(a)+a²Var(b), Var(d)=Var(t)+Var(i); use normal delta-method intervals/tests and preserve independently available total components. Reuse the reference ±2 Mb/MHC and labelled hypothetical covariance sensitivity machinery where reported; disclose assumptions without claiming a geographic window establishes LD independence or empirical covariances are identified.

Apply BH separately to available unique-pair Q tests, available pooled mediator tests, and available indirect/residual component tests. Show tested/unavailable denominators; no claim of joint error control across families. Heterogeneity indicates incompatibility under assumptions, not a uniquely identified direct pathway. Missing components remain unavailable rather than zero.
